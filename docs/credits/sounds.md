## Not sourced

No acceptably licensed recording was found for **hippo**, **rhino** and **walrus**. The only candidates on Wikimedia Commons ("… sound effect.wav", uploaded as CC0 by a single account; the hippo and rhino pages claim "own work", are uncategorized and give no recording details) were rejected because their provenance could not be trusted. A Commons copy of a black swan call labelled CC BY 4.0 was also rejected: its xeno-canto source page (XC674008) states CC BY-NC-ND.
